Prions are misfolded proteins that induce misfolding in normal proteins; they resist standard sterilisation (autoclaving at 134°C, formaldehyde, ionising radiation) and cause invariably fatal neurodegenerative disease with incubation periods of decades. Rabies has a case-fatality rate above 99% once symptoms begin; hydrophobia and aggression are direct results of the virus targeting limbic circuits. Cotard's delusion ('walking corpse syndrome') is a documented neuropsychiatric condition linked to disrupted face/emotion processing; PET studies have shown reduced metabolic activity in frontal and parietal regions of affected patients.

⚠️ Medical topics presented for education. Rabies is preventable with prompt post-exposure vaccination — if bitten by an unknown animal, go to a hospital the same day.
