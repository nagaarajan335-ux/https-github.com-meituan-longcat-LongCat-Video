## Photo tips (so the site looks expensive)

- **Square or 4:5**, product centred, plenty of clean background.
- Best backgrounds: plain white, soft ivory, or a light studio sweep —
  the card crops to the top 38% of the frame, so keep the pack high-ish.
- 1200–2000 px wide, JPG, roughly 200–600 KB each. No watermarks from
  other sellers, no price stickers, no distributor names.
- Same angle/lighting across all 15 makes the catalogue look like one shoot.

## Missing a photo?

If a file isn't there yet, that product simply falls back to its
rendered 2D tile / 3D model — nothing breaks. Add the photo later and it
appears on the next push.
