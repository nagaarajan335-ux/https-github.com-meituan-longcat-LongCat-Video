The Dyatlov Pass incident killed nine experienced skiers; the 2019-2021 Russian review concluded a slab avalanche, but injuries inconsistent with an avalanche and traces of radiation on clothing keep the case contested. The Yuba County Five (1978) remains one of America's strangest disappearances — a functioning car abandoned in a storm, and a survivor found in a mining shed surrounded by untouched food. The Bennington Triangle refers to five disappearances near Glastenbury Mountain, Vermont, including a search plane lost with ten aboard in 1950.
