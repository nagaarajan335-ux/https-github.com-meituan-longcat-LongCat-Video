The ingredient

What exactly is Ganoderma?

Ganoderma lucidum — lingzhi in Chinese, reishi in Japanese — is a wood-decay fungus that has been recorded in traditional Chinese medicine for thousands of years. It grows as a flat, lacquered-looking bracket with a reddish-brown cap.
