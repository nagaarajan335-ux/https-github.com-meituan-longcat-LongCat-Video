The Third Man Factor is documented in survivors of extreme expeditions, including Ernest Shackleton's 1914 Antarctic crossing and solo climbers in death zones. The uncanny valley effect was named by Masahiro Mori in 1970; evolutionary theories link the revulsion response to predator and corpse avoidance. The call of the void was studied by Jennifer Hames and colleagues (2012), who found it occurs most in people with HIGH survival instinct — it is a misread alarm, not a wish.

⚠️ Educational horror. If intrusive thoughts about self-harm are persistent and distressing, please talk to a professional — the 'call of the void' in this video is the brief, universal phenomenon, not suicidal ideation.
