Sleep paralysis occurs when REM atonia persists into waking; the 'intruder' hallucination (hat man, old hag, shadow person) is reported in nearly every culture, which is why researchers treat it as a hard-wired brain response. Fatal Familial Insomnia is a real inherited prion disease (first described in Italy, 1986) with a median survival of ~18 months and no cure. The hypnic jerk affects up to 70% of people; leading theories involve the brainstem misfiring as heart rate and temperature fall at sleep onset.

⚠️ Educational horror. Occasional sleep paralysis is common and harmless. Chronic episodes deserve a sleep doctor, not a ghost hunt.
