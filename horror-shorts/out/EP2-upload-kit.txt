UVB-76 (4625 kHz) has broadcast a monotone buzz since the late 1970s, occasionally interrupted by coded voice messages in Russian; its 2010 silence and return are documented by thousands of shortwave listeners. The Taos Hum affects roughly 2% of residents of Taos, New Mexico and has resisted every acoustic survey. Vic Tandy's 1998 paper 'The Ghost in the Machine' measured 18.98 Hz infrasound in a 'haunted' laboratory — the resonant frequency of the human eyeball — and Richard Wiseman later replicated haunted-space effects with infrasound in a Coventry cellar.
